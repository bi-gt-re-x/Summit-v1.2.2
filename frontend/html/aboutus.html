About Us

Summit is a study and productivity tracker built around a simple idea: progress you can see is progress you'll keep making.

Our Story

Summit started as a personal project — a student's answer to the feeling of working hard all day and still wondering, "did I actually get anything done?" Spreadsheets were tedious, big productivity suites were bloated, and nothing made consistency feel rewarding. So we built the tool we wanted: tasks, a calendar, focus sessions, and goals, all feeding into streaks, XP, and growth ratings that turn effort into something you can watch climb.

What We Believe

⚖ Consistency over Intensity

Small daily wins beat rare all-nighters. Streaks reward showing up.

📈 Measurable Progress

Every action turns into a number you can see and improve.

🏆 Rewarding Productivity

XP, levels and grades make finishing work feel good.

✨ Simplicity First

No clutter. Just the few tools that actually move the needle.

How It's Built

Summit runs on a clean, dependable stack — Python and Flask on the backend, hand-written HTML, CSS and JavaScript on the frontend, and charts drawn with Canvas and SVG. Your data stays local, in plain JSON, where you can always see it. No accounts on distant servers, no subscriptions, no ads.

Where We're Headed

Summit is under active development, and every feature is tested the same way it was born: in real study sessions. If it doesn't help you focus, finish, and level up, it doesn't ship.
